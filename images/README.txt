ITEM PHOTOS GO IN THIS FOLDER
==============================

1. On GitHub, open this folder, press "Add file" > "Upload files".
2. Pick your photo. Name it with small letters and no spaces,
   like  jerky.webp  or  pine-candle.jpg
3. Press "Commit changes".
4. In stock.edn, add a line to the item:
       :image "images/jerky.webp"

Tips
- Keep photos small: about 800 pixels wide is plenty.
  (Big phone photos make the page slow to load.)
- .webp, .jpg and .png all work.
- Capital letters matter. "Jerky.webp" and "jerky.webp" are different.
